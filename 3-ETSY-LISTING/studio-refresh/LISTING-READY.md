## Description — paste from here

Build your resume. Organise your next move.

Career Hub Resume Studio is a resume builder and job search tracker with 3 professional resume templates and a built-in career playbook. Keep your experience, applications, contacts and interview preparation in one browser workspace.

WHAT YOU GET

• 3 professional resume layouts: Executive (one column), Mint Modern (two columns), and Professional Sidebar (a coloured right column). The two-column styles offer five resume accent colours.
• An Experience Bank for work achievements, projects, education, skills and certifications. Choose relevant content for your resume and review the live preview.
• PDF export through your browser’s Save as PDF print option.
• A Job Tracker with Wishlist, Applied, Interviewing, Offer and Rejected stages, plus salary notes, application details and follow-up dates.
• A cover letter editor with matching resume styles, your contact details, optional tracked-job details, editable text and PDF export through browser printing.
• Awards & Honours, editable resume sections and drag-and-drop ordering. Move sections between the two-column layouts, or use arrow buttons.
• A Contacts section for recruiters, mentors and professional conversations.
• 12 Golden Rules, plus practical guidance on achievement bullets, STAR interview stories, networking and reviewing offers. Add your own preparation notes.
• Editable application follow-up drafts to review, copy and send through your own email app.
• Light and dark modes, five workspace accent choices and focus mode.
• JSON backup and restore to keep a copy of your workspace and move entries between devices.

START IN THREE STEPS

1. Add your experience, achievements and profile.
2. Choose a resume layout and the content that fits the role.
3. Save and review your PDF, apply through the employer’s process, and record your progress in the tracker.

FOR YOUR NEXT CAREER MOVE

Useful for students, graduates, job seekers and career changers. Include relevant study projects, volunteering and part-time work as well as paid experience. Reuse your professional record as your experience grows.

YOUR DOWNLOAD

Career-Hub.zip contains Career-Hub.html, the Start Here PDF guide and the personal-use licence. A separate Start Here PDF is also provided. This is a digital product; no physical item is shipped.

OPENING AND USING THE APP

A computer with a current browser is recommended for setup and resume printing. Download from Etsy in a web browser, unzip the package, and open Career-Hub.html. No Career Hub account or app subscription is required. The downloaded app works locally without an internet connection.

The layout adapts to smaller screens. Opening downloaded HTML files and saving PDFs on phones and tablets varies by device and browser; use a computer for the simplest setup. This is a browser app rather than an Excel spreadsheet, Notion template or Word document. Word/DOCX export is not included.

For resume export, choose Save as PDF in your browser’s print dialog, use 100% scale, disable headers and footers, and enable background graphics. Review every page and follow the employer’s requested file format.

YOUR SAVED WORK

Entries stay in your browser’s storage on your device. Use the same browser and app location, and export a JSON backup after important changes. Clearing browser data can remove saved entries. There is no automatic cloud sync; use backup and restore to transfer your workspace. A resume PDF is not a workspace backup.

The 12 Golden Rules help with preparation; they do not promise interviews or hiring. The app’s readiness percentage tracks setup completion, not resume quality or hiring chances. Applications and follow-up messages are sent outside the app. Compatibility with every hiring system is not guaranteed.
