## Listing description — ready to paste

Career Hub is a job search tracker and resume builder app that keeps your applications, experience, and interview preparation together.
Create a tailored resume, save it as PDF through your browser, and keep your next follow-up in view.
For students, graduates, professionals, and career changers — a digital browser app with no account or app subscription required.

A CLEARER PLACE FOR YOUR NEXT CAREER MOVE

When your experience is in one document, your applications in a spreadsheet, and your interview notes somewhere else, keeping track takes extra work. Career Hub brings those details into one workspace you can update as your search progresses.

Build your Experience Bank once. Choose the details that fit each opportunity. Record where you applied, who you spoke to, and what to do next.

FOR STUDENTS & GRADUATES

Your first resume can include more than paid jobs. Add relevant study projects, volunteering, part-time work, education, and skills. Use the handbook’s examples to describe your contribution clearly, then organise internships, graduate opportunities, and entry-level applications.

FOR JOB SEEKERS

Keep each opportunity’s status, application date, salary notes, recruiter details, and follow-up together. Select the experience you want in each resume and prepare examples before an interview. Open your dashboard to see recorded follow-ups and a suggested next step.

FOR PROFESSIONALS & CAREER CHANGERS

Keep a reusable record of your roles, achievements, training, and projects. Bring relevant strengths forward when applying in a new field, or choose a more concise set of experience for an established career. Pick the resume layout that suits your content and the employer’s instructions.

WHAT’S INSIDE CAREER HUB

COVER LETTER — MATCH YOUR APPLICATION
Use your contact details, choose tracked-job details if useful, and write a personal opening, evidence and closing. Preview in either resume style and save as PDF through your browser. Your current draft saves in the workspace.

AWARDS & EDITABLE SECTION ORDER
Add Awards & Honours. Drag resume sections or use arrow buttons to reorder them. Mint Modern supports moving sections between columns. Use Edit in the builder or preview to update content. Name and headline remain at the top.

🏠 HUB — YOUR SEARCH AT A GLANCE
See application, interview, offer, and contact counts, plus upcoming application follow-ups. A suggested next action highlights incomplete setup, a due application follow-up, or a wishlist opportunity. The resume-readiness checklist tracks completed setup steps; it is not a hiring score or an assessment of your resume’s quality.

🗂 EXPERIENCE BANK — YOUR PROFESSIONAL RECORD
Keep your profile, work experience, projects, education, certifications, and skills together. Add achievement bullets and update them as you gain experience. Reuse that information when selecting content for a resume.

📄 RESUME BUILDER — TWO LAYOUTS, YOUR CONTENT
Choose Executive for one column, Mint Modern for two columns, or Professional Sidebar for a coloured right column. Include or exclude experience and projects, choose the available sections, and review your live preview. All three templates use plain-text contacts and standard headings. Mint Modern offers five resume accent colours.

Use the PDF action to open your browser’s print dialog, then choose Save as PDF. The export uses the preview’s design, with page breaks handled by the browser. Review the saved pages before sending. Word/DOCX export is not included.

📌 JOB TRACKER — FROM WISHLIST TO DECISION
Organise applications across five statuses: Wishlist, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, and Rejected. Record the role, company, location, dates, salary notes, contact, and your own comments. Update the status as things change; the board records your progress rather than predicting an outcome.

👥 CONTACTS — KEEP CONVERSATIONS CONNECTED
Record recruiters, mentors, and professional contacts, together with conversation notes and next-contact dates. Keep the context you need for a thoughtful follow-up.

📚 PLAYBOOK — PRACTICAL PREPARATION
Read 12 concise Golden Rules and short guides on achievement bullets, experience beyond paid work, STAR interview examples, networking conversations, and reviewing offers. Add your own notes so preparation reflects your real experience. Advice is written as a starting point you can adapt to the role.

✉️ FOLLOW-UP DRAFT — REVIEW, COPY, SEND
Create an editable application follow-up draft using recorded details. Review the wording, copy it, and send it through your own email app. Career Hub does not send messages or apply for jobs automatically.

🎨 APPEARANCE — MAKE THE WORKSPACE COMFORTABLE
Switch between light and dark mode, choose Indigo, Emerald, Amber, Rose, or Slate accents, and use focus mode to reduce navigation clutter. Sticky header controls stay within reach while you scroll. The layout adapts to smaller screens.

💾 BACKUP & RESTORE — KEEP A COPY OF YOUR WORK
Export your workspace as a JSON backup and restore it when needed. Use this to move entries between devices; there is no automatic cloud sync. A saved resume PDF is a document, not a workspace backup.

TRY THE DEMO FIRST

Explore temporary fictional examples across several professional fields, including applications, achievements, contacts, and preparation notes. Exit sample mode to return to your own workspace. The examples are for learning the app and should be replaced with your own facts.

YOUR DIGITAL DOWNLOAD

You receive:
• Career-Hub.zip — the browser app, Start Here PDF guide, and personal-use licence.
• Career-Hub-Start-Here.pdf — a separate copy of the setup guide.

This is a digital download. No physical planner or other item will be shipped. It is a standalone browser app, not a spreadsheet, Notion template, or Word document.

GETTING STARTED

1. Download your purchase from Etsy in a web browser.
2. Unzip Career-Hub.zip on your computer.
3. Read the Start Here PDF and open Career-Hub.html in your browser.
4. Explore the demo or start adding your own experience and applications.
5. Export a backup after important changes.

DEVICE & PDF DETAILS

A computer is recommended for initial setup and resume printing. The app needs a current browser with JavaScript and local storage enabled. Its layout adapts to phone and tablet screens, but opening local HTML files and saving PDFs varies by device and browser.

For a resume PDF, choose Save as PDF, use 100% scale, turn headers and footers off, and enable background graphics for template colours. Check all pages before submitting. Follow the employer’s requested format and layout; hiring systems may read different layouts differently.

HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED

Your entries are stored in your browser on your device. No Career Hub account is needed. Use the same browser and app location for your workspace, avoid private browsing for saved work, and keep regular backups. Clearing browser storage or changing devices without a backup can make saved entries unavailable. Keep backup files somewhere you trust because they contain your information.

## Saved support replies

**How do I open it?**
Download from Etsy in a web browser, unzip the files on your computer, and follow the Start Here PDF. Open Career-Hub.html in a browser rather than a text editor. Etsy currently does not support downloading digital purchases through its app.

**Where is my saved data?**
Check that you are using the same browser and app location as before, outside private browsing. If you have a backup, use Settings → Restore backup. If browser storage was cleared and no backup exists, the app may not be able to recover the entries.

**How do I save a PDF?**
Use the resume PDF action, then choose Save as PDF in the browser print dialog. Use 100% scale, turn headers and footers off, and enable background graphics for colours. Review all pages before saving.

**Does it sync between devices?**
There is no automatic cloud sync. Export a JSON backup from the original device and restore it on the other device. PDF exports are resume documents, not restorable workspace backups.

**Can I use it for a team?**
The current licence covers personal and household use. It does not include team collaboration or shared workspaces. Contact me about your needs before buying for a business team.

**Can I get a refund?**
Please message me with the issue so I can help. Digital downloads generally cannot be returned, but issues with delivery or a product that is not as described should be handled under Etsy’s policies and any applicable consumer rights. Do not promise that every refund request will be refused.
