Your first minute

  1. Experience Bank: add your profile, roles, education and skills once. Every document reuses them.
  2. Resume Builder: choose from 3 layouts, 4 colours and 2 header styles. Adjust fonts, text size, spacing and A4 or US Letter.
  3. Review: follow the completion and formatting checks, then arrange or hide sections for each role.
  4. Download PDF: choose Save as PDF, scale 100%, headers and footers off.
  5. Cover Letter & Tracker: write a matching letter, add the job to your Tracker and set a follow-up date.

Just exploring? Go to Settings → Load demo data. Clear it anytime with Remove all data.

The one rule: your data lives in your browser

Career Hub saves privately in the browser you use. Open it from the same browser and file location (or the same link) each time. Your computer file and online link keep separate workspaces — move between them with Settings → Export backup and Restore backup. Export a backup weekly and before changing devices.

Troubleshooting

"My entries are gone!"

You probably opened Career Hub in a different browser, moved the HTML file, or used private browsing. Open it the way you did before, or restore your latest backup.

New phone or computer?

On the old device: Settings → Export backup. On the new one: open Career Hub → Settings → Restore backup.

PDF cut off or showing extra text?

In the print window choose Save as PDF, scale 100%, turn headers and footers off and keep background graphics on.

Which layout for application portals?

Choose Executive (one column), then copy the text from your PDF to confirm it reads in the right order.

Dark mode or a different accent?

Desktop: the moon and palette buttons at the top. Phone: More → Appearance.
